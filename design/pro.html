1. Light

White panel, a light grey rail and one deep blue for everything you can act on. The quietest and most conventional of the three, in the vein of mapping software used in an office.

2. Slate and field green

A dark slate rail anchors the brand, the working panel stays white, and a single field green marks actions and selections. It reads as an ag-tech product rather than a generic map tool.

3. Dark

Graphite throughout, so the imagery and overlays carry the colour. The main action is a white button, and water blue is used for toggles. This is the usual look for GIS and drone-mapping tools.
