A. Field book

Styled after a surveyor's waterproof level book. Ruled paper, a red margin line and coloured index tabs that stick out over the map. Checkboxes are marked with a red pencil X. It feels like notes a scout made walking the field.

B. Survey map

The menu becomes the margin of a USGS topographic sheet, with a double neatline and tick marks around the map. The legend swatches are the layer switches. A small compass shows which way the field falls, and soils carry their map-unit symbols the way the printed soil surveys did.

C. Cab display
