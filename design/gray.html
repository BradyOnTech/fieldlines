1a. System grey

Built from Apple's grey ramp and the grouped-list layout of the Settings app. A light grey panel holds white rounded groups with hairline separators, and system blue marks anything you can tap. The fonts are Apple's system fonts (San Francisco on a Mac or iPhone).

1b. Frosted grey

The same panel as frosted glass, like the macOS sidebar or Apple Maps cards. The imagery shows through faintly, which keeps the map feeling like one surface.
