1. Cyanotype and flagging tape

Blueprint blue with a faint drafting grid and white linework, like a printed field plan. Everything that needs your attention is the fluorescent pink of a survey stake's flagging tape.

2. Infrared

Colours from colour-infrared aerial film, where healthy crops show up red and water and bare soil show up cyan. The panel is a dark plum, actions are infrared red, and water and soil are cyan. It reads well at dusk and on a dim screen.

3. Elevation tints

The rail is a map's elevation legend, stepping from bottomland green up to ridge-top brown. The open section turns white and joins the panel like a tab. The panel itself is plain white, with water blue for actions.
